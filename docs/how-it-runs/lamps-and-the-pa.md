# The lamps and the PA

## The lamps

Under the view are the driver's warning lamps. Each is lit by the five-minute move, and each says **ON** or **OFF** in words as well as light.

| Lamp | Lights when | Reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hold on** | the price moves **more than 12%** in five minutes, either way | Rough track |
| **Buffet car** | the price **rises more than 8%** in five minutes | Trolley rolling |
| **Emergency brake** | a five-minute **fall of about 81%** or worse | Brake applied |
| **Brace** | a five-minute **fall of about 94%** or worse | Heads down |

Hold on and Buffet car are advisories and light blue. Emergency brake is a **caution** and lights amber; Brace is a **warning** and lights red. Instead of just lighting up, both fill their whole key and pulse.

## The shake

A five-minute move of **more than 25%**, either way, shakes the whole view. It is cosmetic, and it is the loudest signal on the page.

## The radio log

The **train radio**, beside your ticket, is the PA written down. It keeps the latest twelve lines and announces:

* **Boarding** when the page opens — _"All aboard. Doors closing."_
* **Your seat** — _"Passenger seated in 8A. STANDARD."_, _"Passenger reseated to 7C. BUSINESS."_, or _"Passenger assigned to the freight car. Mind the step."_
* **Worlds** as you cross them — see [The five worlds](the-five-worlds.md).
* **Cautions** — _"Emergency brake applied. Hold on to something."_, then _"Brake released. The line has levelled off."_
* **Your advert** going up on your seat.

Between those, it carries chatter from along the train every few seconds. The chatter is colour, not news — the lines above are the ones that report something real.

## Sound

Sound is **on** unless you have turned it off, and it starts with your first click, tap or key — no browser makes a sound before that. **Sound on / Sound off** in the view's control bar switches it, and the choice is remembered. With it on you hear:

* the train on the rails, looping,
* the **horn** now and then on the move,
* the **long-and-short horn** when the market couples a carriage on,
* the **crossing bell** when the market uncouples one,
* a chime when the Hold on lamp lights.
