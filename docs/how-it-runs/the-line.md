# The line's tiers

The market cap decides how long the train is, and also what the line runs through. There are seven tiers so far. The first three are on the ground and change the country beside the line. From $1M the line leaves the ground altogether. Crossing into a tier changes the whole world outside, and the PA says so.

| Market cap | Tier | What the line runs through | On the PA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| under $100K | **Countryside** | Farmland, forest, mountains, desert and coast | _"Out into open country. Fields both sides."_ when you drop back into it |
| $100K | **Market Town** | Paved streets and lawns, houses, shopfronts on the line with signs on their roofs | _"Now entering Market Town. Shops on both sides of the line."_ |
| $400K | **The City** | Glass towers on a street grid, lit at night, and a river bridge between stations | _"Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the City. River bridge ahead."_ |
| $1M | **Above the clouds** | A viaduct over a sea of cloud | _"We are on the viaduct. Cloud deck below us."_ |
| $10M | **Space** | A glowing guideway, a planet far below | _"Guard to driver: the sky has run out. Sky is black."_ |
| $50M | **The moon** | Craters, boulders and a black sky | _"Ladies and gentlemen, we are now arriving at the moon."_ |
| $100M | **Mars** | Red plains under a butterscotch sky | _"Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Mars. Mind the gap, and the dust."_ |

{% hint style="info" %}
**A tier is entered at its line and left a little under it.** The train moves up into the town at $100K, but only drops back to the country once the market falls 8% below that, to $92K. A market sitting right on a line therefore doesn't flick the world outside (and the PA) back and forth.
{% endhint %}

## Countryside — under $100K

![The Seat Railway train running through farmland](../.gitbook/assets/levels/1-country.jpg)

The line runs through country that never repeats. Every kilometre or so it changes:

* **Farmland**, with patchwork fields, hedgerow trees and farmhouses.
* **Forest**, dense with conifers.
* **Mountains**, where rock shows through and the high ground is under snow.
* **Desert**, with stepped mesas, boulders and cactus.
* **The coast**, where the land falls away to the sea on one side and red channel buoys rock on the swell.

Each one blends into the next. The hills are real relief, and the line curves gently through them.

Every couple of kilometres the train runs through a **Seat Railway station**: a platform under a canopy, with signs giving the market cap and the next carriage. Billboards stand beside the line every few hundred metres, carrying the holders' adverts (see [Every seat is a billboard](../the-wall/every-seat-is-a-billboard.md)).

## Market Town — $100K

The country gives way to a town:

* **Streets:** a grid of streets with dashed white lines down the middle, with paved kerbs and lawns between them.
* **Houses:** each block has houses on it, with gardens and trees.
* **Shops on the line:** the blocks nearest the line hold rows of four- and five-storey shop buildings, with glass fronts, a cyan fascia and billboard frames on their roofs.
* **After dark:** the shop windows and the rooftop signs light up.

The paving uses a real concrete texture with a normal map. The street grid, kerbs and road markings are drawn per pixel, so they stay sharp however far off they are.

## The City — $400K

The town gives way to the city:

* **Towers:** glass and stone towers on a wider street grid, tallest downtown near the line and lower toward the edge of the city.
* **Skyline:** the tall towers step back as they rise, from a podium on the street to a shaft to a narrower top. Their roofs carry antenna masts with red aircraft-warning lights that blink after dark. Lower roofs have plant boxes and old-style water tanks on legs.
* **Facades:** each tower is one of three styles (a grid of punched windows, long ribbon windows, or tall glass between fins), with a glass lobby lit at street level. At night some of the tallest light a crown round their top, cyan or white.
* **Street lamps** line both sides of the track, in the town and the city.
* **Windows:** every tower's windows are cut to its real size, one floor every 3.7 m. At night a share of them light up, warm or cool, never the same pattern twice.
* **Plazas and parks:** some blocks are left open as plazas and small parks.
* **The river:** halfway between every pair of stations the line crosses a river. The valley drops away under a steel suspension bridge with a single tall tower, and the water moves beneath it.

The line runs straight and level over every river crossing in every tier, so the bridge is always ready when the city is.

## Above the clouds — $1M

![The train on a viaduct above a sea of cloud](../.gitbook/assets/levels/2-clouds.jpg)

The line climbs onto a **viaduct** over a sea of cloud. Concrete piers drop away into the cloud tops, and the sky above is a deeper blue.

## Space — $10M

![The train on a guideway through space](../.gitbook/assets/levels/3-space.jpg)

The ground falls away altogether. The train runs on a **guideway through space**, its rails glowing cyan, with the stars out and a planet far below.

## The moon — $50M

![The train crossing the cratered moon](../.gitbook/assets/levels/4-moon.jpg)

Grey regolith, craters with raised rims, boulders, and a black sky full of stars. There is no air, so there is no haze, and Earth hangs in the sky.

## Mars — $100M

![The train crossing the red plains of Mars](../.gitbook/assets/levels/5-mars.jpg)

The furthest the line runs so far: red plains and boulders, mountains on the horizon, a dusty butterscotch sky and two small moons. By $100M the train is thirteen carriages long.

## Where you are right now

* The label at the top of the view names the current tier, for example **Outside · Market Town**.
* **The line** is the map under the view. It lights up as far as you have come, shows the train where it is, rings the next stop (_Next stop: The City at $400K_) and shows how far you are towards it. It is measured on a log scale, so going from $1M to $2M moves it as much as going from $5M to $10M. It doesn't stop at Mars: it carries on, dashed, to a stop that has no name yet.
