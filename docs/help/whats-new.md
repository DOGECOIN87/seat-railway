# What's new

## Version 2

### Runaway, the new game

The landing's game is now **Runaway**: a train with no brakes, three parallel tracks, and left and right to switch between them. Dodge oncoming trains, standing wagons, rockfalls and buffer stops, and pick up tokens on the way. About twenty seconds in the brakes go, and from then on it gets faster until you hit something. It replaces the station-stop challenge, and has its own leaderboard. → [Runaway](../the-game/runaway.md)

### The line has tiers

Under $1M the ground itself now changes with the market. **Countryside** under $100K, **Market Town** from $100K (streets, houses and shop buildings by the line), and **The City** from $400K (glass towers lit at night, and a river crossed on a suspension bridge). Then the viaduct, space, the moon and Mars as before. → [The line's tiers](../how-it-runs/the-line.md)

### Real weather, drawn properly

* **Rain** streaks past at speed and leaves the streets and rails wet and glossy.
* **Snow** settles on everything that faces up.
* **Storms** throw forked lightning.
* **Fog** brings drifting mist.
* **The sea** swells, and turns white-capped in a storm, with channel buoys rocking on it.
* **Inside**, drops run along the coach windows, and the cab's windscreen has working wipers.

→ [The sky outside](../how-it-runs/the-sky.md)

### A train all the way through

The last airline words are gone from the site: the driver and second driver, the line speed, the Top drivers board, and the server's own messages. The seat pages now describe the train's real layout: 118 seats, 2+2, four First Class suites. → [Coaches and seats](../the-train/coaches-and-seats.md)

### Its own token, and its own Helius key

Seat Railway will have its own token. Until it launches, the site is connected to no token at all (it no longer runs on the Seat Airlines one), and one command switches it on. The Worker reads holders through a Helius key of the railway's own. → [The token](../getting-started/the-token.md) · [Configuration → Helius](../for-developers/configuration.md#helius)

## Version 1

Seat Railway began as a copy of [Seat Airlines](https://github.com/DOGECOIN87/Seat-Airlines), with the aeroplane replaced by a train: the length of the train, the line it runs on and the world it runs through all read from the market, 118 seats ranked by holding, the advert wall and the section network.
