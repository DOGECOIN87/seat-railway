# Quick start

### 1. Open the site

Go to [dogecoin87.github.io/seat-railway](https://dogecoin87.github.io/seat-railway/). The page opens on the train, full screen, running through the country with as many carriages as its market cap has earned. The split-flap board turns through the line's sayings — _Hold more, ride longer_ first. Press **Enter** to go in.

### 2. Get the token

The contract address is on the strip across the very top of the page, marked **CA**. **Copy** copies it exactly, and the **pump.fun** button opens the token's page. See [The token](the-token.md) for how to check it.

### 3. Check in with a wallet

Open **Check in** in the tab bar and press **Connect wallet**. Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and Nightly all work. Connecting shares your public address and nothing else — see [Connecting a wallet](connecting-a-wallet.md).

### 4. Take your seat

You do not pick a seat: your holding does. If you are among the 178 biggest holders, the camera walks you to your seat, the PA announces it, and your ticket fills in. If you are not, you ride in the freight car, and the **Boarding ladder** under the check-in card tells you what the last seat aboard is holding.

Once you are seated, the Boarding ladder shows who is directly ahead of you and how many more tokens it takes to pass them.

## Then

* **Look around.** Walk the train from the driver's cab to the freight car — [Views and controls](../the-train/views-and-controls.md).
* **Put an image on your seat.** Find your seat on the wall and press **Advertise here** — it shows on the trackside billboards too. See [Put an advert on your seat](../the-wall/put-an-advert-on-your-seat.md).
* **Publish a networking card.** Sign in to the directory once and publish — [Cards and sign-in](../section-network/cards-and-sign-in.md).

{% hint style="success" %}
Checking in, advertising and networking cost nothing. The only things your wallet is ever asked for are plain-text signatures — see [Wallet safety](../safety/wallet-safety.md).
{% endhint %}
