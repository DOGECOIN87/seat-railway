# Your ticket

Your ticket is in the **Check in** section, and it fills in the moment you are seated. It is built to be screenshotted.

| Field | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Passenger** | Your wallet address, shortened — or **Standby** before you check in |
| **Train** | SR350 |
| **Seat** | The seat your holding earned, such as **8A** — or **—** in the freight car |
| **Class** | DRIVER'S CAB, FIRST, BUSINESS, EXIT ROW or STANDARD — or **STANDBY** |
| **Boarding group** | 1 for the driver's cab and First Class, 2 for Business, 3 for the Exit Row, 4 for Standard |
| **Boarded at** | The market cap when you were seated on this visit |

**Boarded at** is the market cap you boarded at, not the live one, and that is what makes a ticket worth keeping: it records the market at the moment you took your seat.

Under the tear line:

* **A barcode drawn from your seat number** — the same seat always gets the same bars, and no two seats look alike.
* **Your coach's line** — see [Coaches and seats](coaches-and-seats.md#what-your-ticket-says). Seats **30B** and **30E** get their own: _Middle seat, last row, by the lavatory. Does not recline. Merch this._
* Without a seat: _No seat claimed. You are on the standby list._
