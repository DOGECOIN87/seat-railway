# Coaches and seats

## The coaches

| Coach | Ranks | Rows | Layout | Seats | Boarding group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Driver's Cab** | 1–2 | — | Driver (**CPT**) and second driver (**FO**) | 2 | 1 |
| **First Class** | 3–10 | 1–2 | 2-2 — seats A B · E F | 8 | 1 |
| **Business** | 11–40 | 3–7 | 3-3 — seats A B C · D E F | 30 | 2 |
| **Exit Row** | 41–52 | 16–17 | 3-3 | 12 | 3 |
| **Standard** | 53–178 | 8–15 and 18–30 | 3-3 | 126 | 4 |
| **Freight car** | 179 and below | — | at the back of the train | unlimited | — |

In a 3-3 row, **A** and **F** are windows, **C** and **D** are aisles, and **B** and **E** are middles. First Class is 2-2, so its **B** and **E** are aisle seats and it has no middles. The cab's two seats keep the IDs **CPT** and **FO**, which the server and every holder's advert already use.

{% hint style="info" %}
The coaches are the seating ranks, not the carriages you see from outside. The number of carriages is the market cap's (see [One number runs the train](../how-it-runs/one-number-runs-the-train.md)); the 178 seats and their order are the same however long the train is.
{% endhint %}

## What your seat changes

These are real differences, not decoration:

* **Your square on the wall.** The wall is laid out front at the top, and front seats are drawn bigger: the driver's cab at 1.5× and First Class at 1.22× the size of the rest. See [Every seat is a billboard](../the-wall/every-seat-is-a-billboard.md).
* **Your view.** A window seat has the window beside it. An aisle seat looks down the aisle with the window a seat away, and a middle seat has a neighbour's shoulder between it and the daylight. See [Views and controls](views-and-controls.md).
* **Who you see in the directory.** Your own coach, and nobody else's. See [Your seat is which room you are in](../section-network/how-far-you-can-see.md).
* **The PA.** The driver's cab can make one announcement a day that the whole train hears. See [Introductions, rooms and the PA](../section-network/introductions-rooms-and-the-pa.md).

## What your ticket says

Each coach prints its own line on your [ticket](your-ticket.md). Apart from the cab's PA, these are humour rather than features:

| Coach | Printed on the ticket |
| --- | --- |
| Driver's Cab | _You have the PA and the horn. One announcement a day. Use it well._ |
| First Class | _Window table. Champagne on every green candle._ |
| Business | _Priority boarding, and first off the train at every station._ |
| Exit Row | _You have agreed to work the emergency door. Sign the message._ |
| Standard | _Seat back and fold-down table. Welcome aboard._ |
| 30B and 30E | _Middle seat, last row, by the lavatory. Does not recline. Merch this._ |
