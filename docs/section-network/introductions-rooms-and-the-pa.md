# Introductions, rooms and the PA

All three need you to be [signed in to the directory](cards-and-sign-in.md).

## Introductions

An introduction is a private message from one holder to another.

* **Who you can write to:** anyone whose card you can read — the people in your own section, and nobody outside it. See [Your seat is which room you are in](how-far-you-can-see.md).
* **How:** open a holder's card in the roster, write in the box — _Introduce yourself…_ — and press **Send**. A draft is kept per holder, so a note half-written to one card does not follow you to the next.
* **Your inbox:** **Introductions** lists what you have been sent. What you have sent a holder is listed above the box on their card.

Only the two wallets on a conversation can read it.

## Rooms

Every coach is a room as well as a rank.

* **You speak in your own coach's room, and only there.** Write in _Say something to Business…_ (with your own coach's name) and press **Post**.
* **You hear your own coach's room, and only it.** A coach's room belongs to the people sitting in it; no other section can read it.
* New messages appear on their own while the page is open.

## The PA

The driver's cab has the PA — the promise printed on its ticket: _"You have the PA and the horn. One announcement a day. Use it well."_

* Only the two cab seats, **CPT** and **FO**, can announce: **Announce**.
* **Each of them gets one announcement in any 24 hours.** Once today's is made, the box is replaced by a line saying when the PA is yours again.
* Everybody aboard hears it — every coach, and the freight car too. The latest five are shown at the top of the hub, under **The PA**.

## Limits

| Limit | |
| --- | --- |
| Messages per wallet | **20 an hour** — introductions, room posts and announcements together |
| Message length | **1,000 characters** |
| PA announcements | **1 in any 24 hours** for each cab wallet |
| What the hub shows | The latest 50 introductions each way, the latest 50 posts in each room, the latest 5 PA announcements |

You cannot send an introduction to yourself.

## Writing a message

Every message box — an introduction, your coach's room, the PA — works the same way:

* It grows as you type, and shows a count toward the **1,000-character** limit, which turns red near the end.
* **Enter** is a new line. **Ctrl+Enter** (**⌘+Enter** on a Mac) sends.
* The button reads **Sending…**, **Posting…** or **Announcing…** while its message is on its way, and a line under the box says whether it went.
