# Welcome aboard

![The Seat Railway train running through farmland](.gitbook/assets/exterior.jpg)

**Seat Railway** is a train run by one number. The train's length, the line it runs on and the world it runs through are read live from the market for its token, so the train on your screen _is_ the chart. On board are 118 seats, and they go to the biggest holders, in order.

Train **SR350** · Express · [seat-railway.space](https://seat-railway.space/)

## The premise

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Market cap is the length of the train** | A carriage is coupled on at every 1-2-5 step from $10K: $10K, $20K, $50K, $100K … up to fourteen carriages at $200M. Lose a milestone and a carriage is left behind. |
| **Market cap is where the line goes** | Open country, then a market town at $100K and the city at $400K; a viaduct above the clouds at $1M, space at $10M, the moon at $50M, Mars at $100M. |
| **The five-minute move is the grade** | A rising market lays the line ahead uphill, a falling one downhill — so the track behind the train is the chart. |
| **Your bag is your seat** | The 118 biggest holders are seated by rank, driver's cab first. Everyone else rides in the freight car. |
| **Seats are finite** | Out-hold the holder in front of you and you take their seat, and they move back. When your seat changes, the PA announces it. |
| **Every seat is a billboard** | A seated holder can put a square image on their seat. It moves with them, and it goes up on the billboards beside the line. |
| **Your seat is which room you are in** | In the directory you see and reach your own coach, and nobody else's. |
